Hi Steve! Here's a simple plan to learn about trains and fix them:

• Study: Set aside about 1 hour a day (like 6–7 PM). Spend 45 minutes reading and reciting from memory, then 15 minutes reviewing flashcards. Do a weekly practice quiz, and look into a community-college diesel or rail mechanics program.

• Career: Train maintenance is a hands-on, in-demand field with good pay. Common starter roles include rail yard mechanical helper, railcar technician, or locomotive mechanic. A 1–2 year trade program or apprenticeship is a great way to get hired.

• Next steps: Research local diesel/rail programs, visit a rail repair shop to ask about helper or apprentice roles, and consider a basic welding or forklift certification to stand out.
